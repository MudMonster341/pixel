# 0020 — The Main Block ground floor follows the official 3D tour, not the older photo

## Context

The Main Block foyer was rebuilt in the premium pass from the owner's own photo (FB-0030). The official
BITS Dubai 3D virtual tour (3DVista, bits-dubai.ac.ae/dubai-campus-virtual-tour/) shows the building as it is
now and contradicts that foyer (docs/research/tour-ground-floor.md, "Differences vs our current foyer").
The owner was asked and chose the tour: "go with the tour version, this is correct".

## Decision
The reception foyer and the two ground-floor wings (map `main-block-g`, generated by
`tools/interiors/`) are rebuilt to match the tour:
- Pale oak plank floor, no runner. A double-height atrium with the lit wordmark on the mezzanine.
- A hexagonal glass terrarium on a round wooden plinth on the central axis.
- **Two** curved white reception desks, one on each side wall flanking the entrance, each with a glass side
  door and a frosted-glass partition.
- The split staircase against the **left side, towards the back** (not on the central axis); two round columns
  at its foot; back-lit portrait totem pillars along the hall; a spiral gold-ring chandelier over the centre.
- Low round red/blue sofas on the left and right back walls.
- **Two long wings:** right = offices to the auditorium and the visitor lounge; left = Admissions and the
  Director's Office, a ramp, then the Sports Complex, clinic and Mini Mart. Library and Career Services off
  the back-left. Wood skirting and the real door style in the corridors.

Kept unchanged: the story. The LUG stall stays "behind the stairs" (it moves with the staircase to the left
back), the Physics Lab (floor 3), ICVL and Room 195 (floor 1) assignments, the quest route, the door and
floor connections, and every story clearance the tests check (tests/unit/story-clearance.test.js). Only what
the tour clearly shows is changed; compass directions and the staircase position are medium confidence (the
tour's camera can only be dragged), so the owner checks the result in the playtest. Upper floors are not
rebuilt now (no deep rebuild until the owner's feedback).

## Consequences
- `tools/interiors/plans.js`, `build-interiors.js` and `npm run interiors` regenerate `assets/maps/main-block-g.json`
  (and neighbouring maps if the stairwell moves); art goes only through the generators.
- Quest routes (`src/objective-routes.js`), cutscene anchors (`src/scripts.js`), ambient and animal placement
  (`src/ambient.js`) and unit tests that name foyer coordinates must be re-checked after the rebuild.
- The foyer description in docs/STORY.md ("foyer per the owner's photo") is superseded by this ADR.
