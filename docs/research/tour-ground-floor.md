# Main Block: reception and ground floor, from the official 3D virtual tour

Researched 2026-10-03 for the birthday sprint (item F, "Reception and ground floor match").
Source: the official 3DVista tour at https://www.bits-dubai.ac.ae/dubai-campus-virtual-tour/ (the same tour
catalogued in [bits-dubai-campus.md](bits-dubai-campus.md) and [bits-dubai-tour-scenes.json](bits-dubai-tour-scenes.json)).
Screenshots in [tour/](tour/) are © BITS, **reference only, never shipped in the game**.

**Was the tour drivable? Yes.** It loads without a login. I jumped between scenes through the page's own
playlist and rotated each 360 view by dragging. No account, no form, no downloads. (Scene loads take 10-20 s.
The page's own `tour` object throws away any camera yaw set from script, so views were taken by dragging;
compass directions below are worked out from landmarks and may be off by a bit. Confidence is marked.)

Terms: "facing in" means standing at the entrance doors looking into the building. LEFT and RIGHT are from that view.

## What the tour shows (facts)

### 1. Exterior and entrance doors ([01](tour/tour-01-entrance-exterior.jpg))
- Wide steps in grey-brown stone up to the doors. Two pairs of tall round beige columns with plain capitals carry
  a deep fascia reading **"BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus"** with a logo at the left end and a red-salmon cornice under it.
- Glass curtain wall between salmon-coloured vertical pilasters. In the middle a glass **vestibule with automatic
  double sliding doors**, a fixed glass panel each side, and a "Reception" hotspot on the door.
- Walls beige with salmon-trimmed windows and railings. This matches what the game already draws outside.

### 2. The foyer: a bright, modern double-height atrium ([02](tour/tour-02-foyer-from-entrance-looking-in.jpg), [07](tour/tour-07-atrium-toward-entrance-wall.jpg))
- **Floor:** pale oak-look wood/laminate planks, long boards, matte to lightly glossy. Not marble. No darker runner.
- **Ceiling and walls:** white. Soft LED cove strips round the ceiling edges and recessed spot lights. Walls are white
  with a thin wood skirting. Light-wood door frames.
- **The focal piece is a glass terrarium**, not a staircase: a hexagonal glass case (about 3 m tall) with plants and a
  small waterfall-like scene, standing on a **round wooden plinth** with a warm glowing LED base and a ring of partner
  names around it. A small wooden lectern/info stand leans beside it. It stands on the central axis, a few metres
  in from the entrance.
- **Mezzanine:** a curved (oval) first-floor balcony runs round the back/upper part of the atrium. Its balustrade is
  white with a gold/wood handrail and diamond lattice. The inner wall under it carries the lit wordmark **"BITS Pilani,
  Dubai Campus"** with the round logo to its left. Potted plants sit along the balcony (the older owner photo shows
  this too).
- **Ceiling feature:** a spiral chandelier made of gold rings hangs in the void.
- **Wall art:** big white diamond-shaped lit panels on the fascia, some with round portraits in blue, yellow and
  orange diamonds (famous scientists). Tall back-lit totem pillars (white with a coloured stripe and stacked portrait
  panels) stand in pairs along the hall, one pair either side of the terrarium and more further back.
- **Seating:** low round and curved sofas in red and blue, with small tables, grouped along the left and right back
  walls. Large plants in black pots.
- **Wall graphic (left back wall):** "ENGINEER the MIND / WORLD of POSSIBILITIES" lettering. There is also an
  "...SERVICES AHEAD!" banner on the left, and two portraits of the rulers framed on the entrance-wall.

### 3. Reception: **two** curved white desks, one each side of the entrance ([03](tour/tour-03-foyer-right-desk-and-entrance-doors.jpg), [04](tour/tour-04-foyer-left-desk-and-corridor-mouth.jpg), [07](tour/tour-07-atrium-toward-entrance-wall.jpg))
- The reception is not one desk. A **white, softly curved, angular-edged desk (solid-surface look) with a pale
  blue-green LED strip along its edge sits against each side wall, just inside the entrance wall**: one on the left, a
  mirror one on the right. Staff stand behind each. Behind each desk is a **glass partition with frosted
  Arabic-geometric (star) pattern**, with a blue band and a red band behind it (offices).
- Above each desk, a **framed portrait of a ruler** on the wall, and the mezzanine balcony above that. A wall-mounted
  LED screen hangs on the inner wall each side of the vestibule.
- Next to each desk, between it and the entrance doors, is a **glass side door** (a full-height glass swing/sliding
  door to the garden) and a big plant in a black pot.
- No sign says "Reception" on the desks in the views; the label comes from the tour's hotspots.

### 4. The staircase: one broad stair on the LEFT side, not on the back wall ([05](tour/tour-05-foyer2-staircase.jpg), [06](tour/tour-06-foyer2-hall-library-career.jpg))
- Confidence: medium on position (worked out from two scenes), high on shape.
- A **wide central flight that divides into two curved upper flights** (a "split" stair). Two round white columns frame
  its foot. The balustrade is white with a wood/gold handrail. Treads are stone-look, the stair foot has a
  brown stone nosing. A dark/wood plain wall closes the space under and behind it, with the hotspot "Classrooms"
  on the landing at the top.
- It is in the **left half of the building, towards the back (about level with and beyond the terrarium)**,
  facing across the hall. In the "Foyer 2" scene, "Reception" (back to the entrance) is straight ahead and the
  stair is a quarter-turn to the left of that, with the "Library" hall further left.
- Beside the stair, on the foot side, are a pot plant and a back-lit totem pillar. Grey door "Library" (a flush
  wooden door) sits in the wall to its left.
- What is behind the stairs: not visible from the tour. The way up leads to the first-floor "Connecting Lobby",
  which is the mezzanine over the atrium ([12](tour/tour-12-first-floor-mezzanine-over-atrium.jpg)).

### 5. Right of the reception: Offices and Auditorium wing ([08](tour/tour-08-right-wing-offices-corridor.jpg), [11](tour/tour-11-auditorium-lobby.jpg))
- Past the right desk a wide opening leads to a long, straight, narrow **office corridor**. White walls, light
  wood floor, wood skirting, white ceiling tiles with recessed lights, an exit sign, black-potted plants.
  Doors are **wooden-framed frosted-glass doors** with small white name plates: Academic Undergraduate Studies
  Division, Student Welfare Division, Deputy Registrar's Office, then others (International Student Services,
  Meeting rooms, Guest meeting room, Director's Office appear in the tour list).
- At the far end of that corridor, the hotspot **"Auditorium & Lounge"**: the **auditorium lobby** has cream floor
  tiles, a peach/salmon lower wall, white upper wall, and dark-wood double doors under a blue banner
  **"AUDITORIUM"** with a "WELCOMES YOU" backdrop. Black potted plants and a water dispenser stand beside
  the doors.
- Next to it: the **Parents-Visitor Lounge** (black leather sofas, orange patterned rug, wood coffee tables with
  flowers, a BITS logo on the back wall, dark wood doors).

### 6. Left of the reception: Sports Complex, Clinic, Admissions ([04](tour/tour-04-foyer-left-desk-and-corridor-mouth.jpg), [09](tour/tour-09-left-wing-corridor-admissions.jpg), [10](tour/tour-10-left-wing-ramp-to-sports.jpg))
- Past the left desk a corridor mouth leads to the **left wing**: a very long straight corridor (white walls, wood
  skirting, wood floor) with **Admissions Office** (wood-framed frosted glass door), then the **Director's Office
  entrance**, and the "Reception" hotspot back to the foyer.
- At its far end the floor changes to grey/white tile, the walls to **salmon/peach lower walls with a
  rail on both sides, and a shallow ramp** with a **central steel handrail** and a tactile strip. The ramp
  leads down to **Telepresence Classroom** and the **Sports Complex** (dark wood double doors).
- **Sports Complex** (reached through the lobby): a Mini Mart (a small shop, white tile floor, shelves, a
  counter, a fridge), the **Prime Medical Centre** (clinic with a "Reception" sign, blue waiting chairs, a doctor's
  room, and an "Exit to facilities and hostels" hotspot), and a **two-storey sports hall** with polished wood
  courts (badminton), a gallery balcony with a white railing, a trophy cabinet, and the table tennis area.
- Career Services (placement) is on the left of the "Foyer 2" area: a corridor with a wood floor, a "Career Services"
  sign, a "WHERE DREAMS..." wall graphic, and a plant. The Intelligent Computing Lab entrance has cream and peach walls,
  white tile floor, big blue "INTELLIGENT COMPUTING" lettering on the wall and a glass door.

### 7. Corridors in general
- Narrow corridors with white walls, **wood skirting and wood-framed frosted-glass doors**, white tile ceiling grid,
  light-wood plank floors in the office and career areas; **cream/grey tile and peach lower wall** in the
  auditorium, sports and lab-entrance parts. Plants in black pots at corridor ends.
- No lifts, no cafeteria and no notice boards showed up in the ground-floor scenes I walked. (The tour lists a Canteen,
  but under the Library block.)

Wings (medium confidence on where they leave the atrium):
```
 LEFT wing (long, straight):   foyer ... Admissions ... Director's office ... ramp ... Telepresence Classroom
                                                                                   \-> Sports Complex lobby -> Mini Mart,
                                                                                       Clinic, Badminton/Table Tennis hall
 RIGHT wing (long, straight):  foyer ... Academic UG Studies, Student Welfare, Deputy Registrar ... -> Auditorium & Lounge
                                                                                   \-> Auditorium lobby + Parents-Visitor Lounge
```

Overall structure: **a central double-height atrium hall with a long straight wing going out from each side**
(left = sports / clinic / admissions, right = offices / auditorium), plus stairs, Library and Career Services off the
back-left. It is a hall with two wings, not a plain rectangle with a front corridor.

## Inference for the upper floors (inference, **not** fact)

I did not walk any upper-floor corridors for this note. What is known: the first floor has a "Connecting Lobby", which is the
mezzanine gallery over the atrium (white lattice balustrade, the wordmark wall, the same pale floor), and it leads to the
50- and 60-seat classrooms and the locker area. Reasoned guesses:
- Upper corridors probably copy the ground-floor **office corridor**: narrow, white walls, wood skirting, white ceiling
  grid with recessed lights, wood-framed or plain glass-panel doors, a plant in a black pot at the ends, an exit sign.
- Labs and classrooms probably have **plain wood/white doors with a room-number plate and a small window**, doors
  repeated along one or both sides, with the **same pale floor**.
- The mezzanine ring (first floor) likely opens on to a corridor on each side that runs over the ground-floor
  left and right wings.
- Upper floors have no atrium void of their own, so the 2nd and 3rd floors are probably plain double-loaded
  corridors with a stair landing at the same back-left spot as the ground floor stair.

## Differences vs our current foyer (clear contradictions only)

Our foyer (`tools/interiors/plans.js` `mainBlockG`, `FURNISHERS.foyer` in `build-interiors.js`) follows the older owner photo
([owner-main-block-foyer.png](reference/owner-main-block-foyer.png)). The tour shows the building as it is now and
contradicts it in these ways:

1. **Floor:** we draw glossy white/grey marble with a darker centre runner. The tour has pale oak planks and no runner.
2. **Staircase position and shape:** we put a wide twin-flight stair on the central axis, straight ahead of the doors, in
   front of the wordmark wall, with the LUG stall behind it. The tour has the stair against the **left side, towards the
   back**, and the **terrarium** on the central axis instead. (The one stair we show is also the first thing the player
   sees; in the real foyer it is not.)
3. **No terrarium:** the central hexagonal glass terrarium on a round wooden plinth is missing from our foyer.
4. **Reception desk:** we have **one** 3-tile desk on the right side near the entrance. The tour has **two**, one on each
   side wall flanking the entrance, white and curved with LED edge and a frosted-glass partition behind each.
5. **Seating:** our sofas sit at the entrance end, left. In the tour the sofas are low round red/blue units on the
   **left and right back walls**, not beside the entrance.
6. **Chandelier:** ours is a tiered gold fixture over the landing. The tour's is a **spiral of gold rings** hanging in the
   atrium void over the central floor. (Position is the main contradiction: ours is over the stairs.)
7. **Columns:** we have four big round columns at the entrance and stair. The tour's atrium has **back-lit portrait
   totem pillars** along the hall; the round columns are only beside the stair foot (two).
8. **Side exits:** we have wall windows left and right. The tour has **glass side doors** next to each reception
   desk, between it and the entrance.
9. **Wings:** we have a single short corridor off the **east** wall to the stairwell, with two closed doors. The real
   building has **two long wings** off the left and right of the hall (offices and auditorium on the right, sports
   complex / clinic / admissions on the left) plus stair, Library and Career Services off the back-left.
10. **Corridor and door style:** our corridor is a plain `intFloorFoyer` strip. The real ones have wood skirting and
    **wood-framed frosted-glass doors**; the sports-side end has **peach lower walls and a ramp with a centre rail**.

Not a contradiction (keep as is): the "BITS Pilani, Dubai Campus" wordmark above a mezzanine railing, the potted
palms, the chandelier idea and the mezzanine itself: all of that is real. The LUG stall can stay "near the stairs" as the story
says, just beside a left-side stair instead of behind a central one.
